# Care Log

A free medication and vital-signs log for caregivers, for Android. No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Patient information stays on your phone.

## What it does

- Several patients, each with their own medications, readings and doctors.
- Medications by time of day (morning, noon, evening, as needed). One medication can be set for more than one time of day.
- Blood pressure, heart rate and urine output readings.
- Doctors for each patient: role, name and phone number. Tap a number to call.
- ER list: a clean current-medications sheet to share by text or email, or to copy, so you don't have to re-enter everything at the hospital.
- Optional NDC lookup: type the NDC printed on a pill bottle and the app fills in the drug name and strength. Always check the result against the bottle.
- Light and dark themes and adjustable text size.
- Privacy lock: the app asks for your phone's own PIN, pattern or fingerprint before opening.

## Install

1. On your Android phone, open the Releases page of this repository and download `CareLog.apk` from the latest release.
2. Open the downloaded file. Android will warn that it is from outside the Play Store. Allow your browser or file manager to install apps when asked.
3. Tap Install, then open Care Log.

Updates install over the old version and keep your data, as long as you install the new file over the existing app. Do not uninstall first: uninstalling deletes all data.

## Privacy

- Everything you enter is stored only on your phone. There is no account and no server.
- Backups are turned off on purpose, because this is health information.
- The only internet use is the optional NDC lookup. It sends only the NDC number you typed, to the FDA's public drug database. Nothing else leaves your phone.
- Keep a copy of each ER list in your phone's notes as a backup.

## Important

Care Log records what you enter. It is not medical advice and does not check doses or interactions. Always confirm medications against the bottles and with the patient's doctor or pharmacist.
